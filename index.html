Gorilla Sandbox

Headset (Quest / WebXR): press Enter VR. Stand up straight. Plant your hands (circles) on surfaces and push or pull to move. Let go to launch. Left stick up/down: arm reach. Left X: calibrate height (stand up straight first). Right thumbstick left/right: snap turn. Right A / B or left Y: respawn.

Desktop: click to capture the mouse. Left click / Right click reach with the left / right hand. Hold a button and move the mouse to pull or push (drag down to reach the floor). Scroll to extend or retract. Mouse moves the camera while no hand is held. Shift / C crouch (needed to reach the floor). [ / ] reach, H calibrate height, Q / E turn, R respawns, Esc releases the mouse.
